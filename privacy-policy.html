Policy

Effective Date: August 6th 2025

At Lit Lambs Resources, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect your name, email address, phone number, and other details you provide through our forms or purchases.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to deliver services, process payments, and communicate with you about updates, offers, and resources.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or trade your personal information. However, for the purpose of processing payments, your information may be securely handled by trusted third-party payment processors such as Flutterwave, Paystack, or others as needed.

These providers collect your payment information directly on their secure platforms. Lit Lambs does not store or have access to your full credit or debit card details.

4. Security

Your data is securely stored and only accessible to authorized personnel.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your data at any time.
